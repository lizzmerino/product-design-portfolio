UX Case Study

KYC Onboarding

RoleSenior Product Designer
Year2022 – Present
TypeFintech · B2B · Compliance
PlatformsDesktop · Mobile web
Context

A fintech platform where onboarding is the only gate to revenue.

Bancoli is a B2B fintech platform for international trade, serving small businesses and solopreneurs who need to move money across borders. Before anyone can send an invoice or receive a payment, they have to pass KYC (Know Your Customer). I owned the end-to-end UX of that experience.

The Problem

A wall of questions, all at once.

KYC meant dozens of required questions: business classification, company details, owners, identity and documents. The original flow showed them as one long form. Users saw everything at once, had no sense of progress, and dropped off before finishing.

And none of those questions could be removed. Every one existed because compliance required it. The solution had to live entirely in how we asked, not in what we asked.

Before vs. after

Same questions. Completely different feeling.

Before: long form
30+ fields · 1 page

Everything visible at once. Users had to scan, scroll and hunt for what was required.

After: one question at a time

One decision on screen. Pick an answer and the next question slides in.

"The challenge wasn't what to ask. It was how to ask it."

The Approach

Click, click, continue.

Inspired by conversational forms like Typeform, I broke the wall of fields into small, single questions. Most of them became a single column of answers, so the answer is one click away. When a list was too long to show (industries, countries), it became an autocomplete dropdown: type a few letters and pick. Questions that allowed more than one answer used the same dropdown as a multi-select, with an OK button to confirm.

The key detail: as soon as you choose an option, the system scrolls to the next question on its own. No “Next” button to hunt for, no moving the mouse back across the screen. Users could also move with the keyboard. A thousand questions started to feel like a quick conversation.

Rewards

Turning compliance into a goal.

Every answer earned points, shown live in the top corner. Completing a chapter added a bonus. At the end of KYC, users could spend those points inside Bancoli.

Combined with the chapter progress bar, it gave people a reason to keep going, and a small reward for finishing something nobody enjoys doing.
