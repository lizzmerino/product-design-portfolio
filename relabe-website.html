Relabe

RoleUI Designer
Year2021–2023
TypeBrand · Website · App

Relabe is a digital platform where publishers can sell their ad revenue and get paid instantly, turning pending earnings into immediate cash flow.

The challenge was to create Sulvo's sister brand: a payment system born from the same platform but built to stand on its own. Relabe needed to feel familiar enough to earn Sulvo publishers' trust, yet distinct enough to exist as an independent product.

01 · Brand

One line that never stops

Everything in the Relabe brand starts with one detail: the line over the "a" in the wordmark. A macron stretches a sound, and that idea of something extended and continuous became the core of the identity.

Relabe exists so publishers get paid without waiting. We wanted the brand to feel like money flowing easily and without interruption, so that small line became the straight, continuous ribbons of the isometric world, linking screens, cards and charts in one uninterrupted flow.

02 · Design System

Built from the smallest pieces up

I organized the Relabe design system following an atomic approach. Foundations define the raw decisions: color, type, spacing, corners and depth. Atoms are the smallest usable UI pieces built on top of them, like buttons, tabs and status icons. Molecules combine atoms into small components with a single purpose, like an alert or a payment status card.

Working this way kept the platform consistent: changing a foundation updates every atom and molecule that uses it.

03 · Foundations

The raw decisions

The primary palette is anchored by Relabe Blue, complemented by light blue tints and high-contrast dark tones. Success, error and warning colors are reserved for status, so color always carries meaning. Typography pairs Open Sans for the interface with Volkhov, a serif, for display headlines.

06 · Illustration System

Isometric illustration style

To give the platform warmth and visual personality, I developed a library of isometric 3D illustrations. The style references architectural and urban environments (cities, buildings, infrastructure) as a metaphor for the publishing ecosystem Relabe operates within. The soft blue-purple palette ties directly back to the brand, keeping illustrations feeling native rather than generic.

07 · Motion Graphics

Animated 3D elements

Key brand moments use animated isometric 3D graphics: looping compositions that bring the illustration system to life across the website and platform. Each animation follows the same visual language: translucent geometry, soft blue-purple gradients, and smooth easing that feels considered rather than decorative.

08 · Website

The flow, live on the website

The marketing site brought the whole system together. Each benefit is paired with an isometric scene where the ribbons carry cards, money and data, and the blue palette leads visitors down the page to a single action: requesting an account.
